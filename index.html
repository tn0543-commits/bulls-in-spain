The Story: The Running of the Bulls

What’s Spain famous for? The Running of the Bulls, of course! The Running of the Bulls, also called the festival of San Fermín, begins on July 6th every year and ends on July 14th. The nine-day-long activity became so well-known after what an American writer, Ernest Hemingway, wrote in his novel The Sun Also Rises in 1926. He described the fabulous experience of watching the bulls run on the street during his trip.

The bull run takes place at 8 o’clock every morning. The first sound of the rocket signals the corral where the bulls are kept has been opened. Six angry bulls are released from the corral after the first rocket is fired. Then runners dressed in white pants and shirts with a red handkerchief around their necks then start running with the bulls. Not until all the bulls are back to the bullring has the running ended. The distance between the corral and the bullring is 825 meters and the average running time is 3 minutes. As the third rocket is set off, it has indicated that all the bulls have entered the bullring. When the last or the fourth rocket is set off, it means the running has finished.

中文翻譯

西班牙以什麼聞名？當然是奔牛節！奔牛節，也被稱為聖費爾明節，每年於7月6日開始，到7月14日結束。這個為期九天的活動，在美國作家歐內斯特·海明威於1926年的小說《太陽照常升起》中寫下相關內容後，變得非常著名。他描述了在旅行中觀看公牛在街上奔跑的極棒體驗。

奔牛活動每天早上8點舉行。第一聲沖天炮響起，示意關公牛的畜欄已經打開。第一發沖天炮發射後，六頭憤怒的公牛會從畜欄中被釋放出來。接著，穿著白褲白衫、脖子上圍著紅領巾的跑者們開始與公牛一起奔跑。直到所有公牛都回到鬥牛場，奔跑才會結束。畜欄和鬥牛場之間的距離是825公尺，平均奔跑時間為3分鐘。當第三發沖天炮被點燃時，表示所有公牛都已進入鬥牛場。當最後一發或第四發沖天炮被點燃時，這意味著奔牛活動已經結束。

Classroom Rules

RULE 1: EVERYONE STANDS UP!
All students must stand up. Do not look at the Smart Board while another group is playing. Every group has the same five events.
規則 1：全體起立！所有學生必須站起來。當其他小組在玩的時候，請不要看電子白板。每組都有相同的五個事件。
RULE 2: THE WHOLE GROUP PARTICIPATES!
When your group is called, all members must come to the front and work together.
規則 2：全組參與！輪到你們小組時，所有成員必須到教室前面一起合作。
RULE 3: WORK TOGETHER!
Discuss the events quickly. Arrange them from FIRST to LAST according to the story.
規則 3：團隊合作！快速討論這些事件。根據故事，將它們從「最先」到「最後」排列。
RULE 4: BE FAST AND ACCURATE!
You have 60 seconds to arrange the events. The fastest group to complete the correct sequence wins.
規則 4：快速且準確！你們有 60 秒的時間排列這些事件。最快完成正確順序的小組獲勝。
RULE 5: NO PEEKING!
Groups waiting for their turn must not look at the Smart Board. Listen to the teacher.
規則 5：不准偷看！等待輪到的小組不准看電子白板。請仔細聽老師的指示。
RULE 6: THE CORRECT ORDER WINS!
Speed alone is not enough. Your group must arrange all five events correctly to win.
規則 6：順序正確才能獲勝！只有速度是不夠的。你們小組必須將五個事件全部排列正確才能獲勝。
